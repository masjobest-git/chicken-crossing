📖 Instructions

How to Play

  • Use the arrow buttons or keyboard arrows to move the chicken.
  • Avoid the moving cars 🚗.
  • Reach the top row to complete Level 1.
  • Click Next Level to start Level 2.
  • Avoid the mine 💣 and the lorry 🚚.
  • Reach the goal to win the game.
